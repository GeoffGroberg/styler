Media

Images will be center aligned in a new block by default. You should use figure tags for your images, which will give you the option of adding a caption.

.left and .right classes can be added to float figures to the right or left. When figures have .left or .right they will pop-out a bit if they are contained within a .readable div. Text wraps around floated images. On smaller screens, floated images become full-width and centered automatically.
